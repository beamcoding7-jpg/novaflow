Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NovaFlow

Explore detailed answers to common inquiries regarding enterprise security architecture, ERP integrations, multi-currency settlement rails, and SLA commitments.

NovaFlow enforces bank-grade security protocols across every layer of the infrastructure. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, while data at rest is secured via AES-256 with automated hardware security module (HSM) key rotation. We maintain continuous compliance with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS Level 1, and GDPR. Our multi-tenant architecture incorporates isolated virtual private clouds (VPCs) with zero-trust network policies, granular role-based access control (RBAC), and immutable audit logging for all treasury operations.

NovaFlow provides native, bi-directional sync integrations with major enterprise ERP platforms including Oracle NetSuite, SAP S/4HANA, Workday Financial Management, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and QuickBooks Online. Our real-time webhooks and RESTful Treasury API enable automated two-way ledger reconciliation, chart of accounts synchronization, and instant payment initiation directly from your existing financial workflow without custom middleware.

We guarantee a 99.99% system availability SLA for all Scale and Enterprise customers, backed by contractual financial service credits. NovaFlow operates on multi-region, active-active cloud infrastructure distributed across Tier IV data centers with sub-second failover capabilities. Our payment routing engine continuously monitors global banking network health and automatically switches to secondary clearing rails in the event of upstream partner latency or downtime.

NovaFlow supports over 140 currencies across 180+ countries, with direct domestic clearing connectivity into SEPA (Eurozone), ACH and FedNow (US), Faster Payments (UK), and SWIFT GPI for rest-of-world wire disbursements. Built-in multi-currency treasury virtual accounts enable automated FX liquidity sweeps, real-time mid-market rate execution, and local-rail disbursements that eliminate intermediary correspondent banking fees.

Most enterprise customers complete technical integration within 10 to 14 business days. You receive immediate access to a dedicated sandbox environment within 24 hours of account creation. For Scale and Enterprise plans, a dedicated Treasury Solutions Architect assists with ERP connector mapping, API authentication, custom approval rule configurations, and parallel reconciliation testing before transitioning to live production payment flows.
